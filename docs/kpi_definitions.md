# KPI Definitions

## Enrollment KPI

Measures the number of patients enrolled in each clinical trial.

Purpose:

* Track recruitment progress
* Compare enrollment across trials
* Identify underperforming studies

---

## Adverse Event KPI

Measures the number of adverse events reported for each trial.

Purpose:

* Monitor patient safety
* Identify high-risk trials
* Support risk assessment

---

## Site Performance KPI

Measures site-level performance using enrollment and participation metrics.

Purpose:

* Evaluate site effectiveness
* Compare study locations
* Monitor operational efficiency

---

## Drug Supply KPI

Measures inventory availability and shipment activity.

Purpose:

* Prevent stock shortages
* Track drug distribution
* Support supply chain monitoring

---

## Trial Risk Score KPI

Measures trial risk based on adverse event frequency.

Risk Levels:

* LOW
* MEDIUM
* HIGH

Purpose:

* Prioritize monitoring efforts
* Identify potentially problematic trials
* Support clinical decision-making
